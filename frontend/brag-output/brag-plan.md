# Brag Plan: Lead Hunter Club

## What is this app?
Lead Hunter Club intercepts real-time buyer-intent signals across Reddit, LinkedIn and niche forums, AI-filters spam, compiles deep intel dossiers with 94% intent scores, and drops verified leads into your dashboard. ~38% reply rate vs 1.2% bulk scraping.

## The angle
Quiet premium hunt film - obsidian ops room intercepts. Not a SaaS demo, an interception sequence. The wolf isn't chasing, it's already positioned. Raw Signal -> Closed Client.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Stop looking for clients." slams full-bleed white on obsidian, hold. Then "Start intercepting them." in italic serif amber #ffb800. Passive -> predatory in 2 lines.

## Key moments (the middle)
- Live signal interception: Lead Feed cards from Reddit / LinkedIn / Forum with replyProbability 92%, 94%, 96% in cyan #00d7fe
- AI Filters Out the Noise: 4-row funnel - pass Reddit Need logo designer, LinkedIn Looking for dev agency / reject Spam bot, Old post closed
- We Build Lead Intelligence: Pain Point High CAC on Shopify, Budget $5k-$10k, Urgency Critical Q2, Intent Score 94% + mint verified #43ed9e
- Pipeline Saved -> Contacted -> Replied -> Closed

## Outro / punchline
"Start Hunting Free" amber CTA on obsidian + "Trusted by 500+ freelancers" + "Free during early access - Credits roll over". Clean hold.

## User flow worth showing
Lead Feed Live Signals -> AI filters noise -> open intel dossier -> reveal contact -> outreach -> reply. Centerpiece is working app, not marketing sections.
